NOVASTACK

Neon block-stacking above the orbital city
On iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, to play full screen and offline.

How to play

Move and rotate the falling blocks to fill rows. Full rows are blasted away. Hold saves a piece for later, the ghost shows where it will land, and the next 5 pieces are shown on the right.

Clear 4 rows at once for a Novaburst. Twist a T piece into a tight gap for a T-spin. Clear rows on back-to-back pieces for a combo, and chain Novabursts and T-spins for a back-to-back bonus.

Keys: ← → move, ↓ soft drop, Space hard drop, ↑ or X rotate, Z rotate back, A rotate 180, C or Shift hold, Esc or P pause.
Controller: D-pad move and soft drop, D-pad up hard drop, A rotate, B rotate back, X/Y rotate 180 / hard drop, LB/RB hold, Menu pause. Menus: D-pad + A, B to go back.
Touch: use the buttons, or on the board: drag sideways to move, tap to rotate, flick down to hard drop, drag down slowly to soft drop, flick up to hold.
