How to play

You are Loo, a fluffy little moonling who runs round and round tiny planets.

Tap (or press a button) to leap off. You fly straight out the way you were running, so time it so you're pointing at the next planet. Get close and its gravity catches you.

Grab star berries on the way. Flag planets are checkpoints. Miss everything and you float back to the last flag, so you can never get stuck.

Later planets move, spin the other way, and some have grumpy spike moons circling them. Reach the rainbow planet to finish.

There are 20 levels in 4 worlds, and each one is a little longer and trickier. Fly through boost rings to speed up, dive into wormholes to skip ahead, and watch flip planets: their arrows flash just before they swap spin. In level 20, land on Grumbo 3 times to win.

New in v1.3 (each can be switched off in Settings):
• Charge leap: hold to charge (watch the ring), let go to leap further and faster. A quick tap is a short hop.
• Nudge: once per leap, tap again (or press an arrow, D-pad or stick) to steer a little. A tap steers you towards the nearest planet ahead.
• Slingshots: skim close past a planet without landing to whip off faster.
• Planet weights: big planets pull hard and turn slowly, tiny ones pull gently and spin fast.
• Jelly bumpers bounce you (and give your nudge back). Mossy planets make Loo run slower.
• Combos: land again quickly to build a multiplier, up to x8.
• Ghost: race a see-through Loo replaying your best run.
• Golden stars: one is hidden off the path in each level. Find 3 in a world to open its bonus level.
• Wardrobe: crowns unlock hats and trails.
• Daily Orbit: a new level every day. Keep your streak going!

New in v1.4 (each can be switched off in Settings; switching the new ones off brings back the v1.3 look):
• Rounder planets with soft shadows, bright rims and a little squish when you land.
• Deeper sky with extra far-off layers. Colours stay bright, and the haze stays light.
• Leap flourishes: a burst when you launch, a ripple when a planet catches you, and a pop on combos.
• Expressive Loo: blinks, flopping ears, little steps, and a face for charging and flying.
• Smoother camera that eases ahead along your run.
• Heading ribbon: a short streak shows where you are flying. It does not aim for you.

New in v1.5 (each has its own switch; turning the new ones off plays like v1.4):
• Snappier leaps: the charge fills faster, and if you are still holding when you land, the next charge starts at once.
• Guided first steps on the first two levels.
• Per-world music.
• Ice planets make Loo run faster. Whirl planets spin the orbit quicker.
• Launch pads fling you onward. Bell moons bounce you and give your nudge back. Crystal shards sit off the main line.
• Lantern Sea: levels 25 to 30, ending with Luma. Land on Luma 3 times.
• Time trial medals and a world map path.
• Moon charms unlock a scarf, cape, bow, lantern, and comet cloak.
• Kinder checkpoints put you back facing the way on, with a brief shield.
• Assist widens the catch a little and shows a glow where the leap points at the next planet.
• Crisp sparkle pops on berries and checkpoints. Loo stays sharp. The fog stays light.

Keys: Space, Enter or ↑ to leap, Esc pause, arrows + Enter in menus.
Controller: A to leap, Menu to pause, D-pad + A in menus, B back.
Touch: tap anywhere.
