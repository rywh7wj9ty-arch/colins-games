Prismfuse

Stack the gems. Read the numbers. Zap the fuses.
On iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, to play full screen and offline.

How to play

Gems fall like any block game: move, rotate and drop them, and fill a row to clear it.

But some gems hide a fuse. Once gems land, each one shows a number: how many fuses are touching it (including diagonals). A gem showing 0 means everything around it is safe.

Press Scan to freeze time and pick a gem you think is a fuse, then Zap it. A right guess turns it into a golden star worth big points. A wrong guess cracks a heart.

If a row clears with a live fuse still in it, it pops and you lose a heart. Lose all 3 hearts, or stack to the top, and the run ends. Every level is faster and has more fuses, and you get a heart back.

Keys: ← → move, ↓ soft drop, ↑ or X rotate, Z rotate back, Space drop, C hold, S scan (arrows + Space to zap), Esc pause.
Controller: D-pad move, A rotate, B rotate back, Y drop, LB/RB hold, X scan, Menu pause.
Touch: buttons, or tap a gem to scan it, then tap again to zap.
