How to play

Move: ◀ ▶ buttons · Arrows/A D · left stick or D-pad
Jump: ⤒ button · Space/W/↑ · Ⓐ (hold for higher)
Grab: GRAB button · Shift/X · Ⓧ / Ⓑ / RB — hold to hang on ropes. In mid-air, hold Grab near an iron hook to swing on a thread, let go to fling.
Pause: ❚❚ · Esc/P · Menu/Start

Collect the embers ✦ and find 3 lost keepsakes ◈. Lanterns are checkpoints. Gloomlings can't stand being stomped on, but don't bump into them. And when the lane goes quiet… run.
