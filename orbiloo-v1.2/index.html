Orbiloo

Loop round the planets. Let go at just the right moment.
On iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, to play full screen and offline.

How to play

You are Loo, a fluffy little moonling who runs round and round tiny planets.

Tap (or press a button) to leap off. You fly straight out the way you were running, so time it so you're pointing at the next planet. Get close and its gravity catches you.

Grab star berries on the way. Flag planets are checkpoints. Miss everything and you float back to the last flag, so you can never get stuck.

Later planets move, spin the other way, and some have grumpy spike moons circling them. Reach the rainbow planet to finish.

There are 20 levels in 4 worlds, and each one is a little longer and trickier. Fly through boost rings to speed up, dive into wormholes to skip ahead, and watch flip planets: their arrows flash just before they swap spin. In level 20, land on Grumbo 3 times to win.

Keys: Space, Enter or ↑ to leap, Esc pause, arrows + Enter in menus.
Controller: A to leap, Menu to pause, D-pad + A in menus, B back.
Touch: tap anywhere.
