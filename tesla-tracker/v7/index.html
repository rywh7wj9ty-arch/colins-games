Formulas used

  • Current price £ = Current price $ × USD→GBP
  • Average buy £ = Average buy $ × USD→GBP
  • Total initial investment = Shares × Average buy £
  • Total stock price = Current price £ × Shares
  • Total profit = Total stock price − Total initial investment
  • Total % gain = (Total stock price − Initial) ÷ Initial
  • Person totals = SUM of their accounts; Grand total = Colin total + Caroline total
