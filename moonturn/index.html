How to play

  • Move — drag anywhere on the left side (keys: WASD / arrows)
  • Jump — hold longer to jump higher (Space)
  • Twist ⟳ — lights up on a swirl pad and turns part of the planet (F / Enter)
  • Camera — drag on the right side or tap ⟲ ⟳ (Q / E)
  • Eye glyphs — stand on one and face the way it points: what you see becomes real
  • Fall in? Time rewinds you to safety. Collect ♪ Star Notes and find the hidden ✦ Moonmoths.
